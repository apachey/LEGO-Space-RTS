## What changed and why

The earlier canon conflated technological civilization with the absence of
biological technology. Official Mothership/Infiltrator promo cutaways contradict
that premise. Phase 02A now defines the shared armored/skeletal/living identity,
source confidence boundaries, infrastructure derivation, worker identity,
construction, motion, materials, effects and audio across the faction.

Original foundation, faction, roster, economy, world and suspended visual
documents are reconciled in place; this is not a one-worker exception or an
instruction to ignore conflicting originals. INDEX records precedence without
reactivating suspended Phase 08 style conclusions. Costs, timings, resource
flows, combat, refits, locomotion, entity rules and gameplay are unchanged.

Root cause of the failed worker: its prompt followed the absolute non-biological
canon and prescribed ordinary mechanical hardware. The new production policy
combines exterior construction with official interior/promo evidence and keeps
literal labels separate from anatomical inference and new adaptations.

## Derived production material

- A shared source/presentation policy is injected into all 12 Alien packets.
- Alien material and semantic construction drafts are reconciled; the Servitor
  now distinguishes internal support/conduits, integrated tool and external cargo.
- Identity/confusion material distinguishes Alien biomechanical technology from
  Martian robotics. The Martian packet changes only its comparison to Servitor.
- Original generated images, their exact prompts/hashes and original generation
  proposals remain historical provenance. A supersession notice prevents those
  old proposals from being mistaken for current non-biological authority.
- Canon approval does not accept new images. Revised Servitor/defense donor
  compositions still need proposal approval and image review; no third attempt
  is authorized for MT-101/MX-71. Solar 2×3 is not generated by this rewrite.

## Regression protection

Existing 12 review/provenance guards remain intact. Four new negative tests
reject a restored absolute biology ban, a visual-inference source promoted to a
literal label, image acceptance hidden inside canon approval, and gameplay
expansion hidden inside the identity correction. Validator also requires the
shared biomechanical guard in every Alien packet.
