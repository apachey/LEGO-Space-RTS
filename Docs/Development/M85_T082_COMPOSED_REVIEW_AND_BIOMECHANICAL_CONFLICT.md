## Primary source investigation

Reviewed 2026-09-13 through background browser page views, not fan MOCs.

- [LEGO BrickMaster, July-August 2007, Imagine Mars Tech spread](https://archive.org/details/brickmaster-issue17/page/n5/mode/1up):
  Mothership cutaway shows internal branching channels and pale bone-like
  structures inside dark armored craft appendages, alongside named technical
  systems. Interpreting those forms as vascular/skeletal anatomy is a visual
  inference, not a claim that every channel is textually named as a blood vessel.
- [LEGO Club US, July-August 2008, Mars Tech 2.0 / ETX Alien Infiltrator](https://archive.org/details/LEGOClubMagazineUS-JulyAugust2008-Miniland/page/n19/mode/1up):
  explicit "Bio-Organic antigravity propulsion drive" label and cutaway internal
  structures establish biological technology, not merely a vaguely curved
  exterior. Scan is low resolution. These are archived official magazine pages;
  archival hosting does not make the illustrated model a fan creation.
- [Original Assault Turret sprite sheet, Spriters Resource](https://www.spriters-resource.com/browser_games/legomarsmissioncrystalienconflict/asset/134216/):
  multiple facing frames show a central ribbed lime/dark column, integrated
  curved lateral weapon-bearing assemblies and spreading lower contacts.
  The compound frame reads as one recombined Alien structure, rather than a
  complete donor fighter bolted to a separate conventional pedestal.
- [Brickipedia Assault Turret page](https://brickipedia.fandom.com/wiki/CrystAlien_Conflict/Assault_Turret):
  front and rear game screenshots corroborate the sprite composition. The
  accompanying article describes three Alien operators; it is secondary
  evidence and does not authorize adding crew to our defense rules.

A Reddit discussion was used only to locate the two magazine scans. A BrickLink
Studio fan reconstruction returned by image search was excluded. Original game
assets are visual evidence; the hosting archive's text is not gameplay canon.
Do not import the original game's costs, staffing or combined targeting rules.

## PROPOSED CANON CONFLICT

Phase 03 ETX Servitor source-fidelity notes require a mechanical,
"never biological" design. Phase 02 similarly says technological rather than
biological and restricts claw forms from becoming biological. The suspended
Phase 08 visual document repeats stronger prohibitions, but suspension of
Phase 08 does not remove the active Phase 02/03 requirements.

The director's clarification and the magazine evidence contradict the absolute
prohibition. A technological civilization using biological technology does not
logically imply a biological swarm army. Those two decisions were conflated.

The failed Servitor prompt followed that conflation: a bolted mechanical core,
utility clamp and non-biological frame were prescribed, while the official
biomechanical interior grammar was omitted. Adding studs did not repair the
wrong source-language premise. The original reason that canon adopted the
prohibition is not established by this investigation.

Recommended bounded amendment, requiring explicit director approval:

> Mars Mission Alien craft and craft-derived infrastructure may be biomechanical:
> armored manufactured hulls, skeletal internal supports, living conduits and
> bio-organic propulsion/energy assemblies grounded in official set and promo
> evidence. Aliens remain a technological fleet civilization. This does not add
> biological swarm gameplay, generic hives, creep, unrelated tentacles, new
> units, balance changes or Zerg-copy architecture.

Do not amend canonical files or affected production packets until approved.
Then reconcile the active faction/roster requirements and derived identity,
construction and animation drafts together; do not patch only one prompt.
