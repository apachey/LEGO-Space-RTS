# M8.5 T082 — Martians material and texture-needs matrix

The accepted M7 role-authored material family remains authoritative. These are production requirements, not generated texture assets and not permission to bake structural detail into maps.

## Shared rules

- Blue and sand-red open construction, translucent Tube routes and visible mechanical actions define Martians; do not restyle them as smooth black/lime Aliens.
- Pumps, legs, runners, clamps, cranes, switches and docking platforms must expose their parent, load path and practical function.
- Lime and other transparent parts do not emit automatically; emission is reserved for route, pressure, sensor, weapon and operating-state signals.
- Silhouette, Tube walls, connections, open platforms, planted contacts and moving mechanisms remain geometry; textures may not fake them.
