# M8.5 T082 — Astronauts material and texture-needs matrix

The accepted M7 role-authored material family remains authoritative. These are production requirements, not generated texture assets and not permission to bake structural detail into maps.

## Shared rules

- Field Systems use rugged exposed blue-gray construction; Mission Systems use cleaner white-orange modular shells. Neither lineage is a tier.
- Silhouette, openings, load paths, wheel/leg contacts, transformation seams and carried payloads remain geometry.
- Orange identifies mission function and equipment; blue transparent areas remain glass or bounded signals rather than universal glow.
- Baked lighting, fake panel shadows, photographic wear and unreadable micro-labels are prohibited.
