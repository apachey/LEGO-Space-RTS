**Role:** Air scout / interceptor

**State:** `DIRECTOR_ACCEPTED` — T084 authorized for Alien Jet only; T084 has not started.

## Production Design Target

Build the completed official LEGO 5617 Alien Jet as shown in the linked visual
target, preserving its silhouette, proportions, source colors and open pilot
arrangement. The director-supplied image is used unchanged as the visual target
for this decision. The official LEGO 5617 instructions remain the construction
authority and establish the source model's parts and connections.

Keep the broad swept black plate, exposed central pilot, two tall flexible lime
arches, two short lateral emitter arms with their small yellow-green tips, and
the compact rear block. Do not add an enclosed canopy, central nose cannon,
large engine pods, extra wings, or invented surface anatomy. LEGO's five-step
build is sufficiently complete for T084 to reproduce the source geometry.
