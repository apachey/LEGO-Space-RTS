The accompanying target image is the appearance authority for this proposal. It establishes a tiny, low, uncrewed hover worker with a compact black crescent hull, two attached curved shell valves, a protected central rib-and-conduit slot, an external empty cargo cradle and one short two-jaw utility clamp integrated into the forward hull. - Preserve the compact low hover silhouette and black hull dominance with restrained opaque lime accents.
- Keep pale ribs and dark-green living conduits inside the protected central slot; no exposed wet flesh, loose tendrils, decorative veins or unrelated creature limbs.
- Keep the cargo cradle empty in the target. A resource is not part of the unit's body.
- Use one integrated short clamp only. It remains visually distinct from a weapon and reaches the work point by settling the hover shell low.
- No pilot, seat, cockpit, humanoid body, legs, wheels, weapons or new gameplay state.
- Preserve the accepted M7 production baseline: readable silhouette and role-authored materials at the 84-cell starting view; final camera/LOD confirmation belongs to T084/T086.
