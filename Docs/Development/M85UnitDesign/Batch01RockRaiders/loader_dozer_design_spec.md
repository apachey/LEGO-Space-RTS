# Loader Dozer — T083 Design Spec

**Stable ID:** `unit.rock_raiders.loader_dozer`
**Target mode:** `SOURCE_LOCKED`
**State:** proposed for director review; not accepted and not authorized for T084.

## Plain-language decision

The normal Loader Dozer should look like LEGO set 4950: four wheels, open brown operator cage, and the broad front bucket. The saw is not its standard appearance.

The saw comes from a separate game-canon decision: official LEGO promo set 1275 (Chainsaw Bulldozer / Chain Dozer) supplies the front-saw identity for the Loader Dozer's researched Cutter Package. Canon defines it as an equipment upgrade for this same unit, not a separate unit. It is acquired after research. The exact way that upgrade fits onto the 4950 chassis is **not approved by this package**.

## 1. Source Evidence

Official LEGO 4950 instructions establish the base target. Page 1 shows the completed unit; pages 21–22 show the bucket linkage and movement. See the [source images](loader_dozer_target_review.html#source-evidence) and [unit-specific source manifest](loader_dozer_source_evidence.json), which records provenance and hashes.

For the cutter decision, the [canon registry](../../../Canon/00_CANON_SET_REGISTRY.md) identifies set 1275 as a small dozer distinguished by a front working saw. Phase 03 of canon assigns 1275 to the Loader Dozer Cutter Package and says the researched package adds a rotating industrial saw. This establishes why the upgrade exists; it does not establish its fitted geometry on the larger 4950.

## 2. Production Design Target — SOURCE_LOCKED

Use the completed official LEGO 4950 shown on instruction page 1 as the target. Reproduce its source appearance closely: low gray chassis, four large equal black wheels, exposed operator beneath the brown protective frame, turquoise details, yellow fittings and the full-width front bucket with its paired lift arms. Keep the bucket in the base target. Add no cutter, armor, turret, enclosed cab, team marker or other exterior geometry in this proposal.

This is a source-locked target because the official set already shows the intended base game appearance. The existing v2 cutter concept remains a preserved, unaccepted exploration of the later upgrade; it is not the Production Design Target and must not replace the bucket in the base model.

## 3. Design Spec for T084

- Match the source proportions and toy-scale LEGO construction language in the official target.
- Preserve the broad bucket, visible paired lift arms, four-wheel stance and rear-weighted open operator cage.
- Do not model the cutter in place of the bucket as part of this base target.
- Keep the T082 socket and movement contract for the bucket presentation. No gameplay, command, timing, balance or research behavior changes are proposed here.
- Treat the separate Cutter Package as an unresolved visual extension: its gameplay role is canon, but its final fitted appearance needs its own director-approved target before that geometry is built.

## Later verification and evidence limits

The official instructions support the bucket and its visible mechanism. They do not show the larger Loader Dozer fitted with set 1275's saw. Exact cutter mounting, clearances and animation remain undecided and belong to the separate upgrade-design review. Existing T082 notes on the unseen opposite-side hose/control placement remain a disclosed evidence gap, not permission to change the silhouette.

The official images are research references, not runtime art. T084 gameplay-camera, LOD and final geometry checks remain later production work. M7 Final outline-on remains the visual-direction baseline.
