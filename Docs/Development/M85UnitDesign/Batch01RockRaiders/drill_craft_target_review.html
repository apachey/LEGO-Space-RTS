What T084 builds

  • Low, open plate-built sled with exposed operator.
  • Two small raised round side pods around the operator deck.
  • Two separate, narrow, elongated saw bars at the front.
  • Source yellow panels, central hazard strip and turquoise grilles.

Visible adaptations

None to exterior geometry or color. The T083 motion proposal for mirrored saw contact and restrained tooth-loop movement is described separately in the Design Spec; it does not alter the target shape.

No single helical drill, rotating saw-bar body, wheels, closed hull, counterweight, added team-mark geometry or emissive side pods.
