# Drill Craft — T083 Design Spec

**Stable ID:** `unit.rock_raiders.drill_craft`

**Production Design Target mode:** `SOURCE_LOCKED`

**Target artifact:** [director review](drill_craft_target_review.html)

**Target image:** [completed 1277 at instruction step 6](References/1277-drill-craft-target-step6.png)

## T084 build target

Recreate the completed 1277 Hovercraft with Ice Saws shown at instruction step
6. Preserve the low, open plate-built sled; exposed operator; central
black/yellow hazard panel; yellow sloped side panels; turquoise grilles; paired
raised round side pods; and two narrow, elongated forward saw bars on their
separate arms. Keep the scale and proportions shown in the source. The unit is
not a conventional craft with one central helical drill.

The crop is source pixels only. It does not add geometry, recolor parts,
retouch the operator or imply unseen construction. Use the complete source
pages and accepted T082 packet linked by the review for assembly context.

## Source boundary and visible game adaptations

The original LEGO instruction pages survive in an archive-hosted scan. LEGO
branding, product number 1277, product code 4132646 and the 1999 LEGO Group
copyright notice appear on the pages, but the PDF is not hosted by LEGO. The
full-page rasters remain unchanged in the source corpus; the Production Design
Target is a direct crop from page 2. Do not treat either image as runtime art.

There are **no visible model-geometry or color adaptations** in this target.
The existing T083 movement proposal places the twin rigid saw bars at mirrored
contacts and animates a restrained tooth-loop treatment while leaving the
round side pods still. That presentation is separate from the SOURCE_LOCKED
exterior and must not turn either bar into a rotating disc or replace the pair
with a helical drill. Selection, health and faction identification remain
existing presentation systems; no new team-mark geometry is part of this
target.

## Materials and detail

- Preserve the source yellow, turquoise, light gray, dark gray, black and
  operator colors; retain the central hazard pattern and grille blocks.
- Keep the paired tool bars long and thin relative to the sled and operator.
- Do not add armor, wheels, a closed hull, counterweight, weapon or rear cargo.
- The underside and concealed attachment details have incomplete view
  coverage. Follow visible plate construction conservatively; do not claim an
  unseen plate cover or connection as source-verified.
- Apply accepted M7 materials at production time without changing the source
  silhouette or adding emissive glow to the side pods.

## Production gates

T084 resolves actual mesh construction, joint clearances, saw pivots, materials
and required LODs while retaining the accepted exterior target. T087 owns final
animation. These checks do not authorize gameplay, command, footprint, timing
or simulation changes. No technical/explanatory schematic is supplied; none is
used as appearance authority.

**Director acceptance:** the completed source-faithful 1277 exterior was
accepted on 2026-09-24. This authorizes T084 for the unit; T084 has not started.
