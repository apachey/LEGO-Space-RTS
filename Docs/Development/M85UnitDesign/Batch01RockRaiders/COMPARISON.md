## Density and scale audit

Source operators define a common minifigure scale. Do not multiply seams,
greebles, pistons or panels to make the early small sets appear more complex.
Drill Craft's small pair of tools must stay much smaller than Loader's Cutter;
Grinder and Crusher share family cockpit size while their supports differ.
Photographs/illustrations are individually framed, not a metrically calibrated
scale lineup. Exact world-space dimensions and numerical LOD budgets are not
fabricated from perspective images; T084 follows accepted T081 coordinates
and measures the chosen source proportions on constructed geometry.

The passenger and loaded-carrier images establish intended completed
appearance, not physical fit. Their uncertainty is disclosed in the sheets.
A missing underside or hidden hinge must never be certified by a raster.

## Palette and surface audit

Keep source-specific gray, brown, turquoise, black, yellow and transparent
areas. The accepted faction palette is an ensemble direction, not a demand to
paint every unit the same ratio. Crew's five outfits remain distinct. Chrome
is local to the tools; brown cage/rotor plastic is not metal. Transparent lime
polymer is not automatically emissive. Team tiles are small authored patches,
never full-vehicle recoloring or an excuse to erase source character identity.

Do not copy source logos, packaging, generated letters or photographed
lighting into game textures. The texture inventory is a plan, not authored
texture deliverables; T084 owns their creation/integration after design review.
