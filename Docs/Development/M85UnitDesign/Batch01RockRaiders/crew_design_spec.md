# Rock Raiders Crew — T083 Design Spec

**Stable ID:** `unit.rock_raiders.crew`

**Production Design Target mode:** `SOURCE_LOCKED`

**Target artifact:** [director review](crew_review.html)

**Target image:** [completed official 4930 set](References/4930-outbox.jpg)

**Source manifest:** [Crew source evidence](crew_source_manifest.json)

## T084 build target

Build the small, foot-moving Rock Raiders worker shown among the five figures
in the 4930 product image. The unit appearance set consists of the five
source-specific Crew looks: Axle, Bandit, Docs, Jet and Sparks. Keep their
individual headwear, face, torso, leg and carried-tool differences. Use the
shared minifigure-scale body and presentation rig described in the accepted
T082 packet. This is one Crew unit identity with five source looks, not five
new gameplay units.

Preserve the source's bright figure colors and simple toy construction. Keep
the head, torso, short legs, hands, and handheld equipment visually distinct;
do not standardize them into one helmet, suit, backpack, weapon, or uniform.
The source image is the appearance target. No new vehicle, armor, permanent
shared backpack, or extra equipment is part of this target.

## Source boundary and game adaptation

The set photograph is an archival image of the completed official 4930 set;
the LEGO design is the target, while the archive host and image authorship are
not represented as LEGO-authored provenance. The image clearly communicates
the five front-facing figures and their equipment. It does not establish rear
printing or every concealed attachment. Do not invent those details: use a
plain continuation of the observed garment color on unseen surfaces.

There is no visible geometry adaptation in this target. In-game selection,
health, work effects and the existing team identity presentation remain
presentation overlays/effects and do not change the source figures. Tool
contact, motion, damage separation and all other rig details follow the
accepted T082 packet; they are not new gameplay behavior.

## Materials and detail

- Preserve the distinct source color blocks and recognizable printed faces
  and torsos visible in the target.
- Recreate source marks as authored model materials/decals where needed; the
  archival photograph is reference only, not a game texture.
- Unseen rear prints, unseen attachments and exact hidden connections are
  unknown. Do not imply they were verified by this image.
- Apply the accepted M7 material direction at production time without
  changing the source silhouette or recoloring the figures as machinery.

## Production gates

T084 must resolve actual mesh construction, shared rig implementation,
tool-contact clearance, hidden attachment choices, material authoring and
required LODs. These are production checks, not gaps to fill with speculative
concept geometry in this package. No gameplay, command, capacity, timing or
role change is proposed.

**Decision requested:** accept this `SOURCE_LOCKED` target and the five
source-specific looks as the appearance set for the single Crew unit identity,
or name a specific look/detail to change before T084.
