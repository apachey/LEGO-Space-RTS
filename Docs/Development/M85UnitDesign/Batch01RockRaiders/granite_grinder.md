## Production Design Target

Use the completed official 4940 on page 1 as the appearance target. Preserve its
source geometry and proportions: two separated long ski feet, paired narrow
supports and cross-bridge, high open brown operator cage, long forward silver
drill, dark central mechanism, yellow/black hazard bands, turquoise panels, and
short rear equipment/counterweight. The exact official image is the target; no
new exterior geometry is proposed.

The target is shown in the
[director review](granite_grinder_target_review.html). Page 24 is additional
Source Evidence for construction, not a replacement appearance concept.

## Design Spec

- Keep the machine tall and narrow over two planted feet; do not turn it into a
  humanoid, four-legged mech, wheeled chassis, or conventional tank.
- Keep the upper body as one longitudinal drill/cockpit/rear-equipment module
  spanning the two foot modules. Retain the gap beneath the bridge and the open
  cage; do not close it with armor.
- Preserve the long forward drill and short rear mass as the dominant balance.
  The drill must read as a broad LEGO-built segmented tool, not a needle or
  realistic metal auger.
- Match source-scale operator proportions. Retain the brown cage, gray and
  dark-gray frame, turquoise and yellow accents, black/yellow hazard bands,
  pale drill and restrained transparent details. Do not spread metallic
  material across the body.
- At reduced detail, retain the two-foot gap, bridge, open cage, long drill and
  rear counterweight. Fine studs, tiny labels and small tool fittings may be
  simplified only where the silhouette and color blocking remain intact.
- **Visible game adaptation:** restrained alternating short steps, with small
  foot lift and minimal upper-body sway. This is a presentation interpretation
  needed to convey the roster's bipedal walker movement; the LEGO source does
  not prove a gait. It changes no static geometry, proportions or gameplay.
  During the existing drill operation, both feet plant and the source drill may
  show the minimal feed/spin needed to express the authoritative operation.
- No added team markings are required by this target. Do not add new armor,
  weapons, lights, antennas, cargo, or a humanoid torso/head.

## Limits and later gates

The official manual does not establish gait timing or mechanical clearances.
Actual foot articulation, drill feed clearance, materials, gameplay-camera
readability and LOD execution remain T084/T087 production checks. They are not
claimed as verified by this design package. Existing gameplay, footprint and
socket contracts remain unchanged.
