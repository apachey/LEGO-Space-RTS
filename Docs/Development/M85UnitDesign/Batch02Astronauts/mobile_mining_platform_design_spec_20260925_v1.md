# Mobile Mining Platform — T083 Design Spec

**Stable ID:** `unit.astronauts.mobile_mining_platform`\
**Faction / role:** Astronauts / adaptive resource extraction\
**Target mode:** `ADAPTED`\
**Director accepted:** 2026-09-25. **T084 authorized for this unit only; NOT STARTED.**

## Production Design Target

One large, tracked, low white-and-orange Mission Systems platform in two
Service-refit configurations. Both views in the director review are the
accepted appearance target, not source photographs or production geometry:

- **Crystal Reaper:** twin oversized front harvesting wheels; two separate
  clamp manipulators; low tracked chassis; operator cockpit and directly
  docked upper spacecraft/processing module with a visible docking seam.
- **Ore Drill:** retain that same chassis, operator, docked upper module, tracks
  and two manipulators; replace only the paired harvesting wheels with one
  compact forward spiral drill on a visible articulated support.

Keep the intake-to-cargo route readable and the upper module directly mounted;
it must not read as a trailer. Preserve open structural gaps and source-scale
brick construction. The white/orange/black color family belongs to Mission
Systems. Do not blend in Alien forms or the rugged blue-gray Field Systems
palette, and do not add weapons, a sealed generic hull, or a separate support
vehicle.

## Source evidence and adaptation boundary

Official LEGO 7645 establishes the tracked Crystal Reaper, twin harvesting
wheels, independent manipulators and detachable upper craft. Official 7648 and
the human mining vehicle in 7693 inform the compact spiral drill and its
articulated support. These donor sets do not depict the exact combined Ore
Drill. The common selectable chassis, configuration relationship and visible
material route are the game adaptation; no new gameplay is implied.

The two accepted target renders are grayscale appearance studies prepared for
comparison. They communicate the approved topology and relative massing, not
exact source geometry, final color, hidden fit, gameplay-camera readability or
production topology. Official color references are linked in the review.

## Director decision

The director accepted the paired targets as one configurable unit: twin-wheel
Crystal Reaper and shared-chassis single-drill Ore Drill, including the directly
docked upper module and two independent manipulators. T084 is authorized for
this unit only and has not started.
