3. Design Spec

  • Keep the two source appearances visibly distinct. Do not average their palettes, wheel sizes, suspension layouts or cockpit forms.
  • Use Field Systems white/blue-gray language for the 7312 appearance and clean white/orange/black Mission Systems language for 7694.
  • Do not add armor, enclosure, new antennae, insignia or invented center-module geometry.
  • Game configurations remain Escort (initial) and Survey. Their visual mapping to these source lineages is not specified by the accepted T082 material.
  • Source books do not demonstrate driving gait; use only restrained wheel rotation and bounded suspension response as presentation, subject to later animation review.
