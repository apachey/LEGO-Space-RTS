# Astronaut Rover — T083 Design Spec

**Stable ID:** `unit.astronauts.rover`<br>
**Target mode:** `SOURCE_LOCKED`<br>
**State:** Director-accepted. T084 is authorized.

## Authoritative Production Design Target

For T084, realize the completed LEGO 7301 Rover shown on page 1 of the official
LEGO instruction booklet **4156314.pdf** (the booklet cover identifies the
7301/7309 Rover). The exact unmodified official PDF is embedded and linked in
[`rover_review.html`](rover_review.html). Its completed-model view at step 7 is
the primary target; the official page, not a T083 drawing, determines shape,
proportion, construction language and appearance.

Preserve its director-confirmed read: very small and compact; open-top/open-seat;
four dominant white spherical / half-dome wheels; a very small light chassis;
an exposed seated astronaut; the characteristic black front bar/boom; and a
short vertical black antenna/mast. Describe its terminal shape only as shown
in the official image; this spec does not interpret it as a dish or add any
other geometry. Do not add a large blue equipment box. Do not enlarge, enclose,
militarize or embellish the model. Unseen rear and underside construction
remains unknown.

The accepted T082 Rover packet contains prose that conflicts with this direct
official image read. This package follows the official image and the director's
explicit correction. It leaves every accepted T082 file and source record
unchanged.

## Game-specific adaptation

No body geometry, proportions, palette, equipment or marking departure from the
official 7301 target is proposed. For the existing passive-survey function,
T084 may add one restrained, temporary scan pulse at the front boom tip while
survey presentation is active. This is a presentation effect only: it adds no
unit geometry, weapon, module or gameplay rule. If that pulse is omitted, the
source-locked model remains complete and recognizable.

Preserve the existing T082 movement contract: four wheels roll from travel,
all contacts track terrain, and the low chassis remains visually restrained.
This is motion presentation, not a new silhouette or simulation rule.

## Production boundaries

- Use the official page 1 completed-model imagery as the appearance authority.
- Do not treat the explanatory SVG as a concept, target, source image or model
  blueprint; it is labelled **NON-AUTHORITATIVE FOR APPEARANCE**.
- Do not infer hidden rear/underside parts or radar articulation.
- T084 owns production geometry, exact attachment/clearance, material
  integration, required LODs and gameplay-camera readability. T087 owns final
  animation quality.
- This design proposal makes no gameplay, balance, footprint or runtime binding
  changes.
