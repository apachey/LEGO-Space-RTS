## Production Design Target

The authoritative appearance target for T084 is the completed LEGO 7301 Rover
shown on page 1 of official LEGO instruction booklet 4156314.pdf, at the
completed build (step 7). The source image is embedded and linked in the
[review page](rover_review.html). T084 must preserve the source proportions and
construction language: very small and compact, open-top/open-seat, four
dominant white spherical / half-dome wheels, very small light chassis, exposed
seated astronaut, characteristic black front bar/boom, short black mast with
dish, and no large blue equipment box. The official imagery is the target;
this explanatory document does not replace or redraw it.

## Design Spec and actual game adaptation

The complete production handoff is the [Rover Design Spec](rover_design_spec.md).
It keeps the unit source-locked and proposes no visible body geometry,
proportion, palette, equipment or marking changes. For the existing passive
survey function only, it permits a restrained temporary scan pulse at the
front boom tip while the survey presentation is active. This is a visual
effect, not new geometry, a weapon or a gameplay rule. Existing four-wheel
travel and contact presentation follow the accepted T082 movement contract.

## Source limits and later gates

The official instructions do not provide a clean orthogonal rear view,
complete underside detail or moving radar mechanism. Do not infer unseen
construction. T084 owns production geometry, clearances, material integration,
LODs and real gameplay-camera readability; T087 owns final animation quality.

## Director decision

The director accepted the exact official LEGO 7301 page 1 completed-model
imagery as the T084 appearance target, with no visible geometry/material
deviations and the restrained boom-tip survey pulse. **T084: AUTHORIZED, NOT
STARTED.**
