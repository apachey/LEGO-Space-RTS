# CANON STATUS & SOURCE CONTROL

This document is the authoritative creative foundation for all subsequent LEGO Space RTS design work.

Phase 00 established the verified source-material registry. It contains **64 released official source entries**:

- Rock Raiders — **18**
- Life on Mars — **27**
- Mars Mission — **19**

Gameplay coverage remains **0 / 64 assigned** at the conclusion of Phase 01. Phase 01 defines the design space into which those sources will later be integrated; it does not assign individual sets to final units, buildings, upgrades, campaign assets, or other roles. The unreleased Mars Mission **7728 Jet Pack** and **7729 Alien Flyer** remain outside released canon.

The following terminology applies throughout the project:

**OFFICIAL SOURCE MATERIAL**  
Verified characteristics of LEGO Rock Raiders, LEGO Mars Mission, or LEGO Life on Mars sets, vehicles, characters, machinery, structures, equipment, creatures, visual language, or other documented material.

**INTERPRETATION / ADAPTATION**  
Game-design conclusions derived from official source material in order to make the themes function coherently as complete RTS factions.

**NEW GAME CONTENT**  
New connective lore, buildings, units, infrastructure, mechanics, environments, terminology, or visual development required to make a complete commercial RTS.

New game content must never be represented as original LEGO canon.

# 1. EXECUTIVE GAME CONCEPT

## Working Game Concept

LEGO Space RTS is a premium stylized 3D base-building real-time strategy game in which four technologically distinct civilizations compete across a contested multi-world frontier.

The game combines:

- economy;
- expansion;
- construction;
- production;
- technology;
- scouting;
- army composition;
- positioning;
- territorial control;
- direct combat;

with the recognizable machines, industrial logic, modular engineering, crystal technology, eccentric mechanisms, and visual identities of LEGO Rock Raiders, LEGO Mars Mission, and LEGO Life on Mars.

It follows the strategic expectations of a classic RTS without mechanically reskinning another RTS.

Faction mechanics must emerge wherever possible from the source themes themselves.

## Player Fantasy

The player does not merely command LEGO-colored military units.

The player operates a complete LEGO Space civilization under pressure.

Each faction establishes bases, moves resources, builds machines, explores hostile environments, adapts territory to its needs, develops technology, and wages war according to a fundamentally different technological philosophy.

## Strategic Fantasy

The central strategic fantasy is:

**territorial engineering.**

At the beginning of a match, most of the battlefield is hostile, neutral, unknown, or economically undeveloped.

During the match, players transform portions of it into:

- mines;
- production centers;
- energy infrastructure;
- transport systems;
- defended territory;
- crystal operations;
- forward bases;
- reconnaissance networks;
- staging areas.

The player's economy, infrastructure, map control, and military force therefore form one connected system.

## Target Feeling

The game must feel:

- adventurous;
- mechanical;
- competitive;
- colorful;
- strategically readable;
- inventive;
- energetic;
- recognizably LEGO;
- recognizably classic LEGO Space.

Players should repeatedly experience:

1. discovery;
2. construction;
3. mechanical problem-solving;
4. territorial ambition;
5. technological escalation;
6. machine warfare;
7. destruction and rebuilding.

## Distinction From Ordinary RTS Games

Faction identity begins with actual source-material machinery and technological fantasy rather than generic military archetypes.

Important design questions begin with:

- How does this civilization mine?
- How does it move machinery?
- How does it construct?
- What does it physically do with crystals?
- How does it cross difficult terrain?
- How do its vehicles transform or interact?
- What does its infrastructure look like when functioning?

Combat doctrine is derived from these answers.

Economy, construction, logistics, mobility, and combat therefore reinforce the same faction identity.

## Distinction From Ordinary LEGO Games

LEGO is not merely an audiovisual skin or comedy layer.

LEGO construction logic is systemic.

Machines visibly:

- assemble;
- detach;
- reconfigure;
- transport equipment;
- manipulate resources;
- repair through reconstruction;
- break apart into understandable components.

Humor exists, but mastery of a functioning strategic machine is the primary experience.

## Elevator Pitch

**A premium LEGO Space base-building RTS where Rock Raiders, Astronauts, crystal-powered Mars Mission Aliens, and Life on Mars Martians build radically different frontier civilizations, reshape hostile worlds, command iconic industrial and transforming machines, and compete for Ore, Energy, and Crystals across a colorful multi-world frontier.**

# 2. CREATIVE NORTH STAR

## Authoritative North Star

> **Every important system must make the player feel that they are operating a recognizable LEGO Space civilization rather than commanding a generic science-fiction army wearing LEGO colors.**
>
> A mechanic belongs in the game when it strengthens meaningful strategy, arises naturally from the faction's source-material machinery or fantasy, remains readable from the RTS camera, and creates understandable decisions for both the player using it and the opponent facing it.

An idea does not justify itself because:

- the faction “needs a tank”;
- another RTS normally has the feature;
- another faction has something similar;
- it would look spectacular in isolation.

## Core Creative Pillars

### 1. Machinery Before Archetypes

Begin with drills, loaders, modular craft, rovers, walkers, excavation machines, crystal technology, transport systems, and unusual LEGO mechanisms.

Determine their RTS role afterward.

### 2. Four Civilizations, Four Ways to Build

Faction asymmetry exists in:

- economy;
- construction;
- infrastructure;
- expansion;
- logistics;
- technology;
- territory;

not merely in combat statistics.

### 3. The Map Is Part of the Economy

Terrain, resources, travel routes, expansion sites, and infrastructure remain strategically relevant throughout the match.

### 4. Readable Mechanical Spectacle

Drilling, loading, transport, transformation, energy charging, construction, and destruction must be satisfying while preserving RTS clarity.

### 5. Source Identity Survives Adaptation

Rock Raiders remain recognizably Rock Raiders.

Mars Mission remains recognizably Mars Mission.

Life on Mars remains recognizably Life on Mars.

Combining the two human traditions into Astronauts does not erase either visual identity.

### 6. Asymmetry With Counterplay

Faction mechanics can create dramatic advantages, but every strategic advantage must be:

- observable;
- understandable;
- interruptible;
- counterable.

### 7. Build, Expand, Fight

The game never becomes either:

- a combat-only tactics game;
- or a construction game with incidental battles.

Base development and military production remain equally important.

### 8. LEGO Adventure, Not LEGO Parody

Humor and charm enrich the experience.

They never undermine danger, competitive clarity, faction identity, or strategic stakes.

# 3. WORLD PREMISE

## Overall Setting

The original source themes represent geographically and historically separated foundations within a larger spacefaring setting.

Their original identities are not overwritten.

As exploration capability, navigation, and long-range scanning improve, previously separated civilizations begin operating across the same broader multi-world frontier.

Exploration ranges overlap.

Resource expeditions encounter settlements.

Crystal operations encounter competing claims.

New travel routes connect regions that were previously isolated.

The result is a frontier in which all four factions can naturally operate without requiring the original themes to have always shared the same immediate setting.

The frontier's final in-universe proper name remains the responsibility of the later World Bible.

## Why the Civilizations Encounter Each Other

Rock Raiders follow major geological opportunities and crystal deposits.

Astronaut expeditions explore, research, establish outposts, and exploit strategically important worlds.

Life on Mars Martians operate settlements, machines, transport systems, and territorial infrastructure within parts of the same expanding region.

Mars Mission Aliens aggressively pursue crystal-rich territory and strategic technological control.

Their operating zones eventually intersect.

Contact produces:

- trade;
- exploration;
- misunderstanding;
- competition;
- temporary cooperation;
- territorial disputes;
- open warfare.

No faction is universally allied with or universally hostile to every other faction.

## Why Conflict Occurs

Major sources of conflict include:

- competing mineral claims;
- crystal access;
- strategic energy sites;
- expansion routes;
- sovereignty;
- threatened settlements;
- disputed frontier territory;
- recovered technology;
- abandoned installations;
- resource scarcity;
- deliberate military aggression;
- defensive responses to foreign expansion.

Conflict therefore does not depend on a permanent simplistic moral division between human and non-human civilizations.

## Why Expansion Across Multiple Worlds Occurs

No single starting territory provides indefinite access to every material required for complete technological development.

Civilizations expand because they require:

- richer mineral fields;
- energy opportunities;
- high-grade crystal deposits;
- strategic transit routes;
- defensible territory;
- new operating locations.

Expansion is simultaneously economic, technological, and military.

## Ore / Metal

Ore and Metal represent the structural material required to construct civilization.

They support:

- machinery;
- structures;
- armor;
- mechanical components;
- industrial infrastructure.

The strategic resource is treated as one gameplay family even when individual factions process or visually represent it differently.

## Energy

Energy represents operational capacity.

It powers or sustains:

- machinery;
- advanced facilities;
- defenses;
- transportation;
- industrial systems;
- research;
- high-energy technology.

Energy is an infrastructural strategic resource rather than merely another mineral pile.

## Crystals

Crystals are the frontier's most strategically valuable advanced material.

Crystal and resource-handling imagery is strongly represented in the Phase 00 registry across Rock Raiders and Mars Mission, making crystals a legitimate shared strategic foundation rather than an arbitrary crossover invention.

Within the game continuity, crystals are high-density technological and energy-amplification materials.

They enable increasingly advanced:

- propulsion;
- industrial processes;
- specialized technology;
- high-energy systems;
- late-game capabilities.

The four factions do not value or exploit crystals identically.

# 4. TONE

The canonical tone is:

**adventurous heroic science-fiction conflict.**

The game is not:

- gritty military realism;
- nihilistic war fiction;
- constant slapstick;
- consequence-free toy play.

## Adventure

Exploration remains visible beneath warfare.

Worlds should suggest things worth discovering:

- caverns;
- unknown geology;
- strange formations;
- settlements;
- crystal fields;
- abandoned infrastructure;
- active volcanic regions;
- frozen zones;
- asteroid facilities;
- ancient or unexplained locations.

## Conflict

Combat has meaningful strategic consequences.

Bases can be attacked.

Expeditions can fail.

Infrastructure can be destroyed.

Machines can be lost.

Territory can change hands.

These events are exciting and mechanically consequential rather than gruesome.

## LEGO Humor and Charm

Humor arises primarily through:

- machine behavior;
- construction;
- minifigure reactions;
- maintenance;
- repair;
- physical interaction.

Humor remains subordinate to gameplay communication.

## Danger

Danger is communicated through:

- collapsing terrain;
- unstable geology;
- lava;
- crystal discharges;
- damaged machinery;
- alarms;
- hostile vehicles;
- environmental threats.

Realistic injury and graphic suffering are not part of the visual language.

## Discovery

Mystery should primarily generate curiosity and adventure.

Even dangerous unexplored spaces should feel inviting to investigate.

## Seriousness

Characters take their objectives seriously.

They care about:

- territory;
- discoveries;
- resources;
- infrastructure;
- their crews;
- their civilizations.

Sincerity allows LEGO charm to coexist with genuine stakes.

## Colorful Science Fiction

Danger never requires visual desaturation.

Bright machinery, alien effects, crystal caverns, Martian settlements, volcanic environments, deserts, and frozen worlds remain colorful while preserving combat readability.

## Heroic Conflict Without Gritty Realism

A damaged machine:

- loses components;
- sparks;
- exposes mechanical assemblies;
- develops visible malfunctions;
- finally breaks apart into recognizable LEGO construction.

It does not become a realistic burnt military wreck.

A threatened base:

- activates warning lights;
- sounds alarms;
- shows frantic machine and crew activity.

It does not present realistic civilian trauma.

Weapons and industrial tools communicate power through:

- scale;
- impact;
- knockback;
- mechanical disruption;
- shattered constructions;
- battlefield consequence.

They do not imitate realistic contemporary warfare.

# 5. FACTION FANTASY

# ROCK RAIDERS

## Official Source Foundation

The Phase 00 registry supports a broad Rock Raiders industrial ecosystem including:

- scouts;
- drilling machines;
- loaders;
- heavy mining vehicles;
- transport;
- headquarters infrastructure;
- tools;
- resource elements;
- Rock Monsters.

## Core Player Fantasy

**Command an engineering expedition that conquers hostile geology through machinery.**

A Raider player should feel that every safe passage, productive excavation zone, and industrial stronghold exists because their expedition physically made it possible.

## Strategic Personality

**Infrastructure momentum.**

Rock Raiders gradually convert controlled ground into highly productive and defensible industrial territory.

Their strongest positions emerge when:

- extraction;
- transport;
- power;
- engineering;
- defense

reinforce one another.

## Societal and Organizational Character

Rock Raiders are an expeditionary industrial organization built around:

- specialists;
- machinery;
- teamwork;
- practical engineering.

Their culture is:

- industrious;
- cooperative;
- pragmatic;
- improvisational;
- mechanically focused.

They are not primarily a conventional military institution.

Their combat doctrine grows from the need to defend and advance industrial operations.

## Technological Philosophy

**If a physical problem exists, build the correct machine and remove the problem.**

Their technology is:

- mechanical;
- durable;
- visible;
- specialized;
- repairable;
- function-driven.

## Resource Relationship

### Ore / Metal

Their strongest natural industrial resource.

Rock Raiders excel at extracting difficult mineral deposits and converting hostile geology into usable industrial territory.

### Energy

Major industrial operations require dependable energy infrastructure.

Economic growth increases the need for operational power.

### Crystals

A premium mining objective and advanced technological resource.

Crystals are important but do not define the entire Raider economy.

## Territory and Expansion

Rock Raiders do not merely occupy territory.

They **develop** it.

Expansion means:

- opening access;
- excavating;
- installing infrastructure;
- establishing power;
- shortening transport routes;
- securing industrial positions.

## Combat Philosophy

Rock Raiders practice **industrial battlefield control**.

Their armies favor:

- durable machines;
- protected advances;
- engineering support;
- deliberate positioning;
- specialized industrial tools adapted to conflict;
- strong use of prepared territory.

Their offensive identity is an expanding industrial operation, not a generic armored army.

## Major Strengths

- strongest sustained terrain interaction;
- excellent difficult-resource extraction;
- durable infrastructure;
- strong positional defense;
- repair and recovery;
- powerful developed territory;
- strong long-term economic momentum.

## Structural Weaknesses

- infrastructure creates strategic commitments;
- major operations depend on logistics;
- specialized machinery does not provide universal answers;
- strategic redeployment is less effortless than for Astronauts or Aliens;
- losing developed industrial territory is costly.

## Emotional Experience

**“This environment was impossible to operate in. Now it is my industrial stronghold.”**

# ASTRONAUTS

## Official Source Foundation

The faction combines two distinct human design traditions.

Life on Mars humans contribute:

- rugged rovers;
- unusual surface vehicles;
- modular exploration machinery;
- solar-oriented technology;
- compact exploratory craft.

Mars Mission humans contribute:

- drilling machinery;
- mining systems;
- command infrastructure;
- dropships;
- fighters;
- transforming vehicles;
- modular mission systems.

Both traditions remain recognizable inside one playable faction.

## Core Player Fantasy

**Command the most adaptable expeditionary space force in the game.**

Astronaut players examine a situation, select appropriate technologies and modules, assemble a combined-arms response, and reorganize when circumstances change.

## Strategic Personality

**Planned adaptability.**

Astronauts do not receive universal superiority.

Their power comes from the ability to construct coherent responses across multiple strategic situations.

Versatility comes from:

- modularity;
- combined arms;
- mobility;
- technological choice;

not from having a statistically superior generic unit for every problem.

## Societal and Organizational Character

Astronauts are a unified human exploration civilization containing two established engineering traditions.

### Life on Mars Lineage

- rugged;
- exploratory;
- experimental;
- field-oriented.

### Mars Mission Lineage

- organized;
- modular;
- high-performance;
- mission-specialized.

They share one strategic organization without becoming visually homogeneous.

## Technological Philosophy

**Design systems that can be reconfigured when the mission changes.**

Astronaut engineering emphasizes:

- modular sections;
- interchangeable functions;
- detachable equipment;
- transport compatibility;
- transformation;
- combined vehicle systems;
- field adaptation.

## Resource Relationship

### Ore / Metal

Reliable conventional structural economy.

### Energy

Standardized and efficiently managed through dependable infrastructure and mobile systems.

### Crystals

Used for advanced high-performance or specialized technology.

Crystals enhance capability but do not define the entire civilization.

## Territory and Expansion

Astronauts establish **forward expeditions**.

Their expansions become operational relatively quickly and can be specialized around their current mission.

They are more adaptable than Raider industrial sites but less naturally entrenched.

## Combat Philosophy

Combined arms and flexible positioning.

Astronaut armies succeed through coherent combinations of:

- mobile vehicles;
- specialized machines;
- aerial support;
- transport;
- ranged systems;
- modular capabilities.

## Major Strengths

- adaptable compositions;
- reliable mobility;
- strong general reconnaissance;
- technological flexibility;
- strategy switching;
- combined arms;
- dependable core RTS functionality.

## Structural Weaknesses

- adaptability carries opportunity costs;
- specialist factions outperform them in their strongest dedicated domains;
- strategic reconfiguration costs time or resources;
- multiple technology directions create sequencing decisions;
- generic units cannot be universally efficient.

## Emotional Experience

**“Whatever the mission becomes, I can reorganize the expedition to meet it.”**

# ALIENS

## Official Source Foundation

Mars Mission Alien material provides:

- black/lime technological identity;
- attack craft;
- transforming machines;
- infiltrators;
- unusual vehicles;
- a major mothership;
- repeated connection to the crystal conflict.

## Core Player Fantasy

**Command an aggressive crystal-powered technological civilization that converts concentrated energy into battlefield momentum.**

## Strategic Personality

**Energy tempo.**

Aliens are the faction best able to transform temporary resource and energy advantage into immediate pressure.

Their economy and combat doctrine are tightly connected.

## Societal and Organizational Character

Alien source material contains comparatively little mundane civil infrastructure.

Their RTS adaptation therefore does not imitate a generic terrestrial city.

Alien organization feels:

- mobile;
- technologically concentrated;
- mission-driven;
- crystal-focused;
- compact;
- aggressive when opportunity appears.

They are a technological civilization, not a biological swarm.

## Technological Philosophy

**Concentrate power, alter configuration, strike where resistance is weakest.**

Alien machinery emphasizes:

- energetic states;
- transformation;
- unusual movement;
- compact high-output systems.

## Resource Relationship

### Ore / Metal

Necessary structural material, but not the faction's defining economic strength.

### Energy

Core operational medium and a major expression of Alien technological identity.

### Crystals

Strategically central.

Crystals allow Aliens to create powerful offensive tempo and advanced energetic capability.

Exact conversion, charging, storage, or activation mechanics are not defined in Phase 01 and belong to Economy & Technology design.

## Territory and Expansion

Aliens create **energetic footholds**.

Their expansions prioritize:

- crystal access;
- strategic energy;
- mobility;
- attack vectors;
- rapid operational value.

They favor exploiting an opportunity before an opponent can comfortably consolidate.

## Combat Philosophy

Alien warfare emphasizes:

- rapid pressure;
- concentrated attack windows;
- unusual mobility;
- changing configurations;
- pursuit;
- sudden local superiority.

They are not a disposable swarm faction.

Alien machines remain individually readable and technologically deliberate.

## Major Strengths

- initiative;
- mobility;
- crystal exploitation;
- rapid offensive escalation;
- local power concentration;
- flexible threat direction.

## Structural Weaknesses

- crystal commitments create vulnerabilities;
- failed offensive tempo creates recovery windows;
- prolonged low-resource attrition is uncomfortable;
- passive fortified warfare is not their ideal state;
- opponents who survive major pressure gain opportunities to retaliate.

## Emotional Experience

**“I find an opening, concentrate power, and force the battlefield to move at my pace.”**

# MARTIANS

## Official Source Foundation

Life on Mars Martian material provides:

- hover vehicles;
- worker robots;
- articulated machinery;
- transforming machines;
- walkers;
- excavation systems;
- Aero Tube infrastructure;
- hypersled and pneumatic transport concepts.

## Core Player Fantasy

**Command an eccentric mechanical civilization that solves strategic problems through strange machinery, transport systems, connectivity, and battlefield reconfiguration.**

## Strategic Personality

**Network manipulation.**

Aliens create advantage through concentrated energy.

Martians create advantage through relationships between:

- machines;
- locations;
- routes;
- infrastructure.

## Societal and Organizational Character

Martians are an established civilization rather than a temporary expedition.

Their engineering is:

- inventive;
- specialized;
- culturally distinctive;
- mechanically sophisticated;
- unconventional.

Unusual machine shapes are treated as functional expressions of Martian engineering rather than visual jokes.

## Technological Philosophy

**Connect the correct mechanisms and the battlefield itself becomes useful.**

## Resource Relationship

### Ore / Metal

Collected and manipulated through mechanical workers and excavation machinery.

### Energy

Distributed and routed through Martian infrastructure.

### Crystals

An advanced technological material, but not the civilization's defining economic obsession.

Their primary economic identity is connectivity and mechanical logistics.

## Territory and Expansion

Martians create **networks**.

A new outpost gains additional strategic value by becoming part of a larger connected Martian system.

Aero Tube and hypersled source concepts are the primary inspiration for this identity.

Exact network transportation rules remain the responsibility of later systems design.

## Combat Philosophy

Martians manipulate battles through:

- repositioning;
- unusual movement;
- utility machinery;
- route control;
- displacement;
- support mechanisms;
- mechanical transformation.

Their strategic advantage comes from making an opponent's position wrong rather than simply producing larger damage numbers.

## Major Strengths

- battlefield manipulation;
- network-based mobility;
- positional utility;
- flexible defensive geometry;
- prepared-territory advantage;
- unconventional support systems.

## Structural Weaknesses

- important systems require setup;
- disconnected infrastructure loses efficiency;
- direct brute-force combat is not a universal solution;
- sophisticated machinery demands strategic understanding;
- disruption of key connections can temporarily weaken operations.

## Emotional Experience

**“My opponent is fighting on the map. I am changing how the map functions.”**

| Strategic Dimension | Rock Raiders | Astronauts | Aliens | Martians |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Economy** | Industrial extraction and developed geology | Reliable balanced economy with modular adaptation | Crystal-sensitive economy that converts advantage into tempo | Connected economy based on efficient routing and infrastructure |
| **Construction** | Machinery-heavy deliberate development | Standardized modular deployment | Compact energy-oriented footholds | Connected mechanical modules |
| **Expansion** | Takes investment to fully develop but becomes highly productive | Fast, dependable forward operations | Aggressive footholds around strategic resources | Expansions gain value through network integration |
| **Mobility** | Specialized transport; industrial empire has inertia | Broad dependable strategic mobility | Strong pressure-oriented mobility | Unconventional infrastructure and repositioning mobility |
| **Durability** | High sustained mechanical and structural resilience | Dependable middle ground | Relies more on tempo than attritional durability | Variable; survival strongly affected by positioning and utility |
| **Aggression** | Methodical territorial advance | Flexible timing attacks | Strongest natural offensive tempo | Indirect positional and disruptive aggression |
| **Defense** | Prepared industrial strongholds | Flexible conventional response | Active defense through counter-pressure | Utility, route control, and mechanical preparation |
| **Terrain Interaction** | Physically excavates and industrializes terrain | Adapts equipment to geography | Uses mobility and energy systems to overcome geography | Alters movement relationships through infrastructure |
| **Technology** | Increasing industrial depth | Increasing modular specialization | Increasing energy intensity | Increasing network complexity |
| **Information** | Exploration tied to mining and route discovery | Strong general reconnaissance | Fast aggressive information acquisition | Information through network presence and unusual machinery |
| **Battlefield Manipulation** | Alters terrain and obstacles | Reconfigures own capabilities | Changes tempo and threat direction | Alters routes, positioning, and location relationships |
| **Logistics** | Strong physical transport and infrastructure demands | Moderate and readable logistics | Resource timing and energetic commitments | Network architecture and routing |
| **Comeback Pattern** | Surviving industrial territory can restore momentum | Composition and technology can be redirected | One successful tempo window can reopen the game | Surviving network nodes can rebuild positional influence |

## Astronauts vs Rock Raiders — Canonical Separation

Both source families legitimately contain:

- exploration;
- drills;
- mining machines;
- surface vehicles.

Their distinction is not based on speed versus durability.

### Rock Raiders transform the environment.

Their machinery forms an industrial ecosystem.

### Astronauts transform their equipment.

Their machinery forms a modular mission ecosystem.

When both encounter a difficult resource location:

Rock Raiders:

**excavate → secure → power → industrialize.**

Astronauts:

**survey → configure → deploy → operate → reposition.**

This distinction must remain visible throughout economy, bases, technology, and combat.

## Aliens vs Martians — Canonical Separation

Both are technologically unusual non-human factions, so their fundamental verbs must remain different.

### Aliens manipulate power and tempo.

Core verbs:

- charge;
- concentrate;
- surge;
- transform;
- strike;
- accelerate.

### Martians manipulate relationships and positioning.

Core verbs:

- connect;
- route;
- reposition;
- redirect;
- carry;
- rearrange.

Aliens are energetic and offensive.

Martians are mechanical and systemic.

# 7. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

## Starting Phase

Each player establishes the first functional economic system.

Opening decisions immediately express faction identity.

Players choose:

- initial resource priority;
- scouting investment;
- economic growth;
- first expansion timing;
- initial production capability.

Openings cannot be mechanically identical across all four factions.

## Early Expansion

Starting territory cannot comfortably sustain the entire game.

Players investigate:

- Ore deposits;
- Energy opportunities;
- crystals;
- defensible positions;
- attack routes;
- future infrastructure.

Expansion creates economic opportunity and strategic exposure.

## Scouting

Scouting must reveal decisions, not merely units.

Important questions include:

- Where is the opponent expanding?
- Which resources are being prioritized?
- Which technology direction is visible?
- Which routes are becoming important?
- Where is infrastructure concentrated?
- Where are crystals being contested?

## First Conflict

Early engagements focus on:

- workers;
- exposed resource operations;
- scouts;
- forward infrastructure;
- expansion denial.

Significant advantage can be created without destroying a main base.

## Mid-Game

Players control multiple interacting economic and military systems.

This is where each faction's macro identity becomes strongly visible:

- Raider industrial territory;
- Astronaut modular composition;
- Alien energy tempo;
- Martian networks.

## Technology Escalation

Advancement unlocks new strategic operations rather than only stronger statistics.

The battlefield gains:

- heavier machinery;
- specialized support;
- advanced mobility;
- stronger siege;
- improved logistics;
- more complex army compositions.

## Territorial Struggle

Mid- and late-game conflict focuses on networks of valuable locations:

- crystals;
- resource fields;
- energy sites;
- routes;
- industrial zones;
- forward positions;
- staging territory.

## Late Game

Late-game armies are powerful but remain recognizable as medium-scale LEGO machine forces.

The game does not become a screen-filling superweapon spectacle.

Advanced warfare increases the importance of:

- scouting;
- combined arms;
- logistics;
- siege;
- positioning;
- reinforcement paths.

## Victory

Standard competitive play uses a common victory framework based on eliminating the opponent's practical ability to operate through destruction of essential bases and production capacity.

Faction-specific victory conditions are not part of standard competitive play.

Alternative game modes and campaign scenarios may later use:

- territorial control;
- extraction;
- resource objectives;
- survival;
- scenario-specific goals.

# 8. ECONOMY FOUNDATION

All factions use the same three primary strategic resources.

Their meaning remains stable across matchups so players can understand comparative economic positions.

Faction asymmetry changes **how** resources move through each civilization.

## Ore / Metal — Construction Capacity

Ore answers:

**“How much physical civilization can I construct?”**

Primary uses include:

- buildings;
- vehicles;
- machinery;
- mechanical upgrades;
- infrastructure.

It is the broadest and most predictable material resource.

## Energy — Operational Capacity

Energy answers:

**“How much technology can I operate?”**

Energy supports:

- infrastructure;
- advanced facilities;
- defensive systems;
- production;
- transport;
- research;
- specialized machines.

Energy must generate meaningful spatial or infrastructural decisions.

## Crystals — Strategic Intensity

Crystals answer:

**“How far can I push advanced technology?”**

They are:

- relatively scarce;
- geographically important;
- clearly readable;
- strategically contested.

## Acquisition

**Rock Raiders:** strongest relationship with difficult geological extraction.

**Astronauts:** dependable acquisition supported by mobile and modular systems.

**Aliens:** unusually strong strategic interaction with crystal resources.

**Martians:** efficient acquisition through mechanical systems and infrastructure connectivity.

## Storage

**Rock Raiders:** physical industrial storage.

**Astronauts:** standardized modular storage.

**Aliens:** storage reflects high-density energy and crystal technology.

**Martians:** storage is integrated into connected mechanical infrastructure.

Exact storage mechanics are defined later.

## Transportation

**Rock Raiders:** resources are visibly hauled through industrial logistics.

**Astronauts:** multipurpose transportation supports flexible expeditions.

**Aliens:** resource handling minimizes conventional logistics where their energy technology credibly supports it.

**Martians:** infrastructure and routing are fundamental to transportation identity.

## Processing

# 10. COMBAT FOUNDATION

## Army Scale

Combat uses medium-sized armies.

Armies must be large enough for:

- formation;
- composition;
- flanking;
- reinforcement;
- screening;

while remaining small enough for individual vehicle classes and mechanical identities to remain readable.

## Readable Engagements

At normal RTS camera distance, players must understand:

- faction;
- movement type;
- approximate durability;
- major combat role;
- major transformation or special operational state.

## Combined Arms

No broadly available universal unit solves every battlefield problem.

Armies benefit from combinations of:

- frontline machines;
- mobile attackers;
- ranged support;
- siege;
- anti-mobile tools;
- reconnaissance;
- support and utility systems.

## Positioning

Positioning matters through:

- range;
- approach direction;
- terrain;
- retreat routes;
- firing geometry;
- reinforcement distance;
- access to high-value targets.

## Focus Fire

Focus fire is valuable but does not define all combat.

System design can regulate excessive focus-fire dominance through later choices involving:

- armor behavior;
- unit size;
- movement;
- screening;
- repair;
- weapon behavior;
- overkill.

Exact formulas are not part of Phase 01.

## Mobility

Movement is a primary faction differentiator.

Unusual movement must remain visible and predictable enough for opponents to respond.

## Siege

Siege represents specialized ability to defeat prepared infrastructure.

Siege systems are:

- powerful against positions;
- strategically expensive;
- vulnerable without support.

## Support

Support does not require every unit to become an activated-ability platform.

Support effects may arise from:

- repair;
- transport;
- scanning;
- power;
- mechanical interaction;
- displacement;
- logistics.

## Counters

Counters are strong advantage relationships, not absolute hard locks.

Poor composition creates disadvantage.

It does not make player agency disappear.

Players can still affect outcomes through:

- terrain;
- positioning;
- reinforcement;
- economy;
- retreat;
- focus;
- scouting.

## Ability Complexity

Most units derive their identity from:

- movement;
- weapon behavior;
- targeting;
- transformation;
- durability;
- passive machinery;
- interaction with other systems.

Only a limited portion of an army should demand constant manual ability activation.

Strategic judgment takes precedence over continuous ability maintenance.

# 15. MULTIPLAYER HEALTH PRINCIPLES

## 1. Major Mechanics Must Be Observable

Players must be able to identify meaningful enemy commitments.

Examples include:

- Raider industrial development;
- Astronaut technological specialization;
- Alien crystal/energy escalation;
- Martian network construction.

Strategic information may be hidden by fog of war.

System rules themselves must not be hidden.

## 2. Scouting Matters

Failure to scout creates legitimate strategic vulnerability.

Correct scouting must provide actionable information.

## 3. Rushes Trade Security for Timing

Early aggression is supported.

No opening should reliably eliminate an opponent before meaningful scouting and response are possible.

## 4. Defense Protects Territory but Does Not Replace Map Control

Strong bases are valid.

A player who permanently abandons the wider map eventually suffers:

- economic;
- positional;
- strategic

consequences.

## 5. Economic Growth Creates Exposure

Greedy expansion creates advantage only if the player successfully protects the investment.

## 6. Harassment Damages Efficiency

Healthy harassment targets:

- workers;
- exposed resource systems;
- logistics;
- power;
- expansion timing;
- scouting.

Individual harassment actions should usually create recoverable damage unless repeated or strategically decisive.

## 7. Late-Game Power Requires Visible Preparation

Major advanced capabilities cannot appear without readable strategic investment.

## 8. No Hard-Lock Matchups

No faction mechanic may create states equivalent to:

- “this faction cannot attack this strategy”;
- “this map is automatically won by this faction”;
- “this composition has no possible response.”

## 9. Signature Mechanics Need Counterplay

### Rock Raiders

Terrain advantages require contestable access points and strategic commitment.

### Astronauts

Adaptation and module switching cannot be instantaneous and free.

### Aliens

Major energy or crystal escalation exposes a meaningful strategic commitment.

### Martians

Network mobility depends on visible, disruptable infrastructure.

## 10. Asymmetry Does Not Replace RTS Fundamentals

Every faction must care about:

- economy;
- scouting;
- expansion;
- army composition;
- production;
- positioning;
- territory.

Signature systems deepen the RTS.

They do not bypass it.

38. The world is presented as a living LEGO-designed universe rather than physical toys on a tabletop.

39. Meaningful official-set representation does not require one competitive unit per release.
40. Small, promotional, accessory, character-only, duplicate, and bundle releases may receive meaningful representation through appropriate gameplay, visual, environmental, UI, or campaign systems.
41. Life on Mars-derived Astronaut content must receive strategically meaningful representation and cannot be reduced to cosmetics.
42. Mars Mission Aliens may require original economy, infrastructure, worker, or production designs because the official range provides less mundane RTS infrastructure.
43. Any invented Alien material must remain visibly derived from Mars Mission Alien design language and cannot become generic biological alien architecture.

### 5. Exact Rock Raider Terrain Rules

The exact categories of excavatable terrain, route creation, blocker interaction, and competitive limitations are not yet defined.

Duplicate and bundle releases remain independently tracked in the Canon Set Registry.

The final rule for when those registry entries count as gameplay “covered” belongs to Canon Set Mapping.

Character-only source material must receive meaningful representation without creating hero-centered gameplay.

Its exact coverage method remains unresolved.

### 8. Detailed Visual Palettes

Precise palette specifications, material ratios, decals, insignia systems, and color-value standards require a dedicated Visual Bible phase using detailed official reference research.
