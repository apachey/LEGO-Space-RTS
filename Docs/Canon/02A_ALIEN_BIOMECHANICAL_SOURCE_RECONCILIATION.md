# Alien biomechanical source reconciliation

Status: authoritative, explicitly authorized by the game director on 2026-09-13.
The director rejected a local exception and requested a coherent canon rewrite
because the previous technological/non-biological distinction was incorrect.

## Faction identity

Mars Mission Aliens are a technological, crystal-oriented fleet civilization
whose craft are biomechanical. Technology and biology are not opposites here.
Manufactured armored hulls, articulated modules and crystal systems coexist
with skeletal internal supports, living conduits and bio-organic assemblies.
They are neither contemporary human robots painted black/lime nor a copied
biological swarm faction. Life on Mars Martians remain a distinct civilization
with their own articulated machinery and pneumatic/network identity.

## Evidence and its limits

- [Official LEGO BrickMaster, July-August 2007, Imagine Mars Tech](https://archive.org/details/brickmaster-issue17/page/n5/mode/1up):
  Mothership cutaway depicts pale bone-like supports and branching channels
  within armored craft, alongside named technical systems. Skeletal/vascular
  interpretation is visual inference; the scan does not textually establish
  that every channel is a blood vessel, nor its fluid chemistry.
- [Official LEGO Club US, July-August 2008, Mars Tech 2.0](https://archive.org/details/LEGOClubMagazineUS-JulyAugust2008-Miniland/page/n19/mode/1up):
  Infiltrator cutaway explicitly labels a “Bio-Organic antigravity propulsion
  drive”. This is direct evidence for biological technology.
- Official building instructions establish exterior modules, LEGO connections,
  pilots, weapon placement and conversions. They do not alone establish the
  fictional nature of every interior component. Promo cutaways complement,
  rather than replace, those construction sources.

These sources establish the shared biomechanical language, not an identical
visible organ layout for every craft. Do not invent detailed anatomy and mark
it SOURCE_VERIFIED merely because the family is biomechanical.

## Craft, workers and architecture

Preserve source-faithful silhouettes and major assemblies of official craft.
Do not add organs, tendrils or creature limbs across an accepted exterior to
demonstrate the correction. Interior design and disclosed new adaptations may
express the living systems wherever function and source evidence support them.

The ETX Servitor remains a new spacecraft-derived economic worker. Its armored
hover shell, internal support/conduit assembly and working clamp must read as
Alien biomechanical technology, not a plausible present-day human robotic arm
on a deck. Exact shell, clamp, organ and connection designs still require a
disclosed donor/adaptation proposal and director review. A biomechanical
identity does not add independent creature locomotion or an attack.

New infrastructure derives from landed/unfolded craft, carrier bays and their
internal technology. Ground contacts, cargo routes, tool mounts and energy
interfaces must connect into a comprehensible compound structure. A biological
component still requires an identified purpose, parent, load path and visible
connection. A whole fighter attached to an unrelated rectangular pedestal is
not sufficient source derivation.

Original LEGO game recombinations, including CrystAlien Conflict's Assault
Turret, are method precedents: recombine recognizable donor assemblies into
one functional structure. They do not authorize copying costs, crew counts,
target classes or entire buildings. Official promotional, unreleased and combo
material may supply evidence with provenance disclosed. Alternate official
builds remain case-by-case; fan MOCs are not an approved design source.

## Motion, materials, effects and audio

Hull articulation retains physical hinges and connected modules. Living
conduits may flex with those modules; restrained internal contraction or
pressure motion is a disclosed presentation adaptation, not proof of a new
simulation mechanism. No panel teleportation or effect hiding a missing joint.

Exterior source materials and the accepted palette remain intact. Distinguish
armored shell, internal supports, living conduits and crystal/energy interfaces;
do not replace them with generic wet skin. Opaque lime is not permanently
emissive. Any anatomical texture, motion or sound needs a source/adaptation
record and review; no new texture asset is accepted by this text.

Construction remains arrive → anchor → unfold → connect → energize. Biological
systems can connect/activate inside that sequence; construction does not become
unapproved gestation or spawning. Destruction remains readable LEGO assembly
failure, not realistic gore. Weapons retain their approved energy behavior;
bio-organic propulsion permits an organic undertone without requiring creature
roars. Spores, slime attacks and insect-swarm effects are not added.

## Unchanged gameplay and acceptance boundaries

No new units, buildings, abilities, resource rules, costs, timings, population,
health, combat values, refit rules or balance. No hive-mind, creep, infection,
gestation, regeneration or swarm-production system is introduced. Alien society
and frontier terrain do not become biological hive territory by inference.
Servitor still harvests Ore/Crystals, builds and repairs Alien structures,
hovers on the ground layer and has no meaningful combat. Ground Pulse remains
anti-ground; Air Lance remains anti-air with existing refit restrictions.
7693 remains exclusively airborne. Mothership remains one selectable carrier.

Previously accepted images and historical generation manifests remain intact.
Unreviewed designs remain unreviewed; canon correction is not image acceptance.
Phase 08's general visual-authority suspension remains in force. Its obsolete
absolute biology bans are corrected for consistency, not reactivated as a style
lock. Complete-roster review and all existing two-attempt stops remain required.

## Production guard

Every Alien production packet must inherit this identity. Combine exterior
construction evidence with official interior/promo evidence when making
biological claims; distinguish literal source labels from visual inference and
new adaptation. Explain a proposed unit/building's gameplay purpose before
showing its concept. A contradiction must trigger source reconciliation, not
blind obedience to an old prompt or an unreported local exception.
