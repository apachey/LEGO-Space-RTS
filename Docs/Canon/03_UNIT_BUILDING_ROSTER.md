# CANON STATUS

Phase 03 converts the four locked faction architectures into complete competitive rosters.

The authoritative released-source denominator remains:

- Rock Raiders — **18**
- Life on Mars — **27**
- Mars Mission — **19**
- **TOTAL — 64**

This phase establishes full meaningful project coverage of all 64 released entries.

It does **not** interpret 64 releases as 64 competitive units.

Exact reissues, bundles, character packs, accessories, environmental elements, and small promotional sources receive coverage in the form most faithful to the physical source.

The unreleased Mars Mission:

- 7728 Jet Pack;
- 7729 Alien Flyer;

remain outside released canon and outside mandatory roster coverage.

# PART II — ROCK RAIDERS ROSTER

## Roster doctrine

Rock Raiders field the smallest conventional roster.

This is intentional.

Their strategic breadth comes from:

- Worksite infrastructure;
- excavation;
- industrial repair;
- terrain access;
- specialized machinery.

Their machines remain mining and engineering equipment first.

---

## ROCK RAIDER UNITS

### 1. ROCK RAIDER CREW

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4930 Rock Raiders Crew; 3347–3349 Mini Heroes character material; crew appearances throughout the main range.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Worker / engineer  
**TECH STAGE:** Early

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Harvest, construct, repair, operate industrial work.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Emergency field servicing and demolition preparation.

**MOVEMENT:** Foot.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Portable mining and engineering tools provide only weak emergency close-range defense.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Benefits from Worksite servicing and automatically uses nearby worksite equipment when performing construction or repair animations. No combat ability bar.

**STRENGTHS:** Flexible economic labor; excellent repair inside developed territory.

**WEAKNESSES:** Vulnerable away from vehicles and infrastructure.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Harassment of exposed crews delays construction, processing, and repair.

**VISUAL READ:** Bright minifigure-scale personnel moving between much larger industrial machines; helmets, tools, lamps, distinctive Rock Raider work gear.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** Characters remain recognizable crew rather than becoming infantry squads. Combat is deliberately secondary to their official worker/explorer identity.

---

### 2. HOVER SCOUT

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-DIRECT  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4910 Hover Scout; 1274 Light Hover contributes the lightweight promotional model variant.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Scout / detector  
**TECH STAGE:** Early

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Reconnaissance.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Geological surveying and route discovery.

**MOVEMENT:** Ground-layer hover. Ignores shallow water, minor rubble, and rough ground; blocked by cliffs, solid rock walls, and normal impassable terrain.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Minimal survey-tool fire; not a serious combat asset.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Passive geological scanner highlights nearby authored Excavatable Terrain Features and improves detection of concealed battlefield objects.

**STRENGTHS:** Fast, cheap information; highly useful throughout the match.

**WEAKNESSES:** Fragile and poorly armed.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Fast interceptors and defensive coverage can remove it easily if it overextends.

**VISUAL READ:** Tiny single-seat hover chassis with broad side profile and highly visible scanner/tool package.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** Its official light scouting identity is preserved almost directly.

---

### 3. DRILL CRAFT

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 1277 Drill Craft.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Engineering utility vehicle  
**TECH STAGE:** Early

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Excavate authored terrain features.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Remove rubble and threaten undefended structures.

**MOVEMENT:** Compact ground machine.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Two small forward ice saws require very short range. Together they perform the same excavation function against rock, rubble, and structures and remain poor against mobile units.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Primary Raider Excavation vehicle.

**STRENGTHS:** Creates routes and industrial shortcuts earlier and more efficiently than combat drilling machines.

**WEAKNESSES:** Weak combat survivability.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Opponents can scout excavation, attack the Drill Craft, or prepare at the future exit.

**VISUAL READ:** Tiny low hover chassis identified by two small mirrored forward ice saws, raised round side pods, and the exposed operator between them.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** The source design's twin-saw tool package is preserved as its defining visual and gameplay function rather than being replaced by a generic drill or miniature combat tank.

---

### 4. RAPID RIDER

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4920 Rapid Rider.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Fast utility transport  
**TECH STAGE:** Early

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Rapid crew transport across difficult surface terrain.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Harassment logistics, scouting support, emergency resource ferry.

**MOVEMENT:** Amphibious surface-skimmer adaptation of the source twin-hull water craft. Excellent across water and rough flat terrain; cannot cross cliffs.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Essentially unarmed. Offensive value comes from delivering Crew or demolition personnel.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Carries a small Crew team and unloads rapidly.

**STRENGTHS:** Cheap mobility and flexible transport.

**WEAKNESSES:** Very low direct combat value.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Intercept it before unloading or cover vulnerable economic areas.

**VISUAL READ:** Distinctive twin-hull silhouette and exposed rider position.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** The aquatic/cargo character of the official model is preserved; limited land-skimming is an RTS adaptation ensuring usefulness on non-water maps.

---

### 5. LOADER DOZER

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4950 Loader Dozer; 1275 Chainsaw Bulldozer / Chain Dozer becomes its cutter equipment package.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Heavy utility / frontline machine  
**TECH STAGE:** Early–Mid

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Durable anti-light frontline presence.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Clear rubble, push light obstructions, support construction zones.

**MOVEMENT:** Heavy wheeled ground vehicle.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Front scoop rams and deflects light targets. Its cutter package adds a rotating industrial saw for sustained short-range anti-light damage.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Cutter Package is a researched equipment upgrade, not a separate unit.

**STRENGTHS:** Durable early line-holder; useful in economy and combat.

**WEAKNESSES:** Very short range; poor against dedicated anti-heavy and air.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Kite it, isolate it from repair, or use heavy-target weapons.

**VISUAL READ:** Huge front scoop or cutter silhouette dominates the machine.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** Remains visibly a loader/dozer. Its fighting behavior is a direct adaptation of industrial contact tools.

### 6. GRANITE GRINDER

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4940 Granite Grinder.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Drill walker / anti-heavy breacher  
**TECH STAGE:** Mid

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Anti-heavy combat.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Structure breaching and difficult industrial excavation.

**MOVEMENT:** Bipedal mechanical walker.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Large forward drill must establish contact and then deals increasing sustained damage to heavy machines and structures.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Automatically braces while drilling a stationary target; movement breaks the drilling cycle.

**STRENGTHS:** Excellent against slow durable targets; terrain-friendly walker movement.

**WEAKNESSES:** Vulnerable to swarms, ranged kiting, and air.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Force it to retarget or move before the drill reaches full effectiveness.

**VISUAL READ:** Tall bipedal profile with enormous horizontal drill.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** The official walker/drill identity is preserved. Military utility derives entirely from the drill.

### 7. CHROME CRUSHER

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4970 Chrome Crusher.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Advanced heavy machine / siege  
**TECH STAGE:** Advanced

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Heavy structure assault.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Break heavily armored vehicles and anchor an Industrial Advance.

**MOVEMENT:** Large wheeled ground machine.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** Massive drill for direct assault, assisted by industrial demolition equipment.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** No separate activated combat trick. Its power depends on being supported long enough to reach the target.

**STRENGTHS:** Exceptional structure pressure; high durability.

**WEAKNESSES:** Expensive, large, slow to redirect, vulnerable to air and concentrated anti-heavy fire.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Flank the support line, attack from range, or force redeployment.

**VISUAL READ:** Largest conventional Raider ground vehicle, huge chrome drill, lights, radar and cargo equipment.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** Remains a mining flagship adapted into battlefield breaching machinery, not a main battle tank.

### 8. TUNNEL TRANSPORT

**ORIGIN:** OFFICIAL-ADAPTED  
**SOURCE SETS / MOTIFS:** 4980 Tunnel Transport; 1276 Helicopter Transport supplies the light cargo-rotorcraft visual family and campaign logistics variant.

**GAMEPLAY CLASS:** Heavy aircraft / transport  
**TECH STAGE:** Advanced

**PRIMARY ROLE:** Strategic machinery transport.

**SECONDARY ROLE:** Forward logistical lift and emergency extraction.

**MOVEMENT:** True air.

**ATTACK / TOOL BEHAVIOR:** No meaningful normal attack.

**SPECIAL MECHANIC:** Can carry one heavy eligible Raider machine or several smaller units. Loading requires a short stationary hover/landing operation.

**STRENGTHS:** Allows otherwise slow Raider machinery to appear on unexpected fronts.

**WEAKNESSES:** High investment; vulnerable to anti-air; carries no major offensive firepower itself.

**COUNTERPLAY:** Scout loading zones, intercept in transit, or cover likely unloading positions.

**VISUAL READ:** Huge twin-rotor/propeller transport silhouette carrying visible industrial cargo.

**SOURCE-FIDELITY NOTES:** The source's transport function remains its entire strategic identity.

### Why the two new Raider defenses are necessary

Official Rock Raiders material provides extensive machinery and HQ infrastructure but no complete competitive static defense family.

**Crusher Barrier** extrapolates directly from loader/dozer machinery and keeps ground defense physical.

**Cutter Mast** supplies mandatory anti-air using mining-cutter and survey technology rather than a generic missile turret.

No additional original Raider combat unit is required.

# PART III — ASTRONAUT ROSTER

## Roster doctrine

Astronauts have the broadest unit roster because their source pool contains two large human engineering traditions.

Roster size is controlled by deliberately combining overlapping source designs into mission families.

Neither lineage is a tier.

**Field Systems — Life on Mars derived:** rugged exploration, surface operation, sustainment.

**Mission Systems — Mars Mission derived:** high-performance mission specialization, aerospace, extraction, transformation.

# PART VIII — DEFENSIVE ARCHITECTURE

## ROCK RAIDERS — INDUSTRIAL FORTIFICATION

Rock Raider defense combines:

- Crusher Barriers;
- Cutter Masts;
- repair from nearby service infrastructure;
- tough buildings;
- useful excavation geometry.

The faction's strongest defense comes from an operating industrial position.

It does not rely on a ring of conventional gun turrets.

**Anti-ground:** Crusher Barrier + heavy machinery.

**Anti-air:** Cutter Mast.

**Counterplay:** siege, multi-front pressure, attacking Energy/service infrastructure.

---

## ASTRONAUTS — MODULAR DEPENDABLE DEFENSE

The Modular Sentinel uses one chassis but requires an explicit loadout.

**Ground Interdiction Module:** anti-ground.

**Air Interception Module:** anti-air.

One Sentinel cannot efficiently cover both simultaneously.

Astronaut defenders therefore configure positions according to expected threat.

**Counterplay:** scout the installed module and attack through the uncovered target class or force costly refits.

---

## ALIENS — ACTIVE ENERGY DEFENSE

ETX Defense Nodes resemble attack craft that have landed and opened into fixed firing frames.

Ground and air configurations are distinct.

Alien defense works best when Nodes buy enough time for mobile forces to counterattack.

It does not become the game's strongest static defense.

**Counterplay:** force the wrong configuration, siege from another vector, or attack Resonance infrastructure elsewhere.

---

## MARTIANS — NETWORKED POSITIONAL DEFENSE

**Deflector Arm:** physically disrupts light ground attackers.

**Aero Guard Tower:** elevated anti-air.

The real Martian defensive advantage is rapid network reinforcement.

A weak local defense can become strong quickly if:

- Tube Stations survive;
- exits remain usable;
- defenders exist elsewhere in the network.

**Counterplay:** simultaneous attacks, Tube isolation and Station siege.

# PART IX — AIR AND TRANSPORT SYSTEM

## 1. TRUE AIR

A **true-air unit** moves in the air layer and normally ignores:

- ground pathing;
- shallow/deep water;
- cliffs;
- ground rubble;
- normal ground chokepoints.

True-air units remain airborne unless their own source-derived transformation explicitly changes their state. ETX Alien Strike has no such transformation.

Examples:

- Tunnel Transport;
- Mono Jet;
- Mission Fighter;
- Recon Dropship;
- Operations Aircraft;
- Alien Jet;
- ETX Alien Strike;
- Alien Mothership;
- Aero Skiff.

---

## 2. HOVER

Hover is a **ground movement class**, not true air.

Hover units:

- remain ground targets;
- are blocked by cliffs and solid walls;
- can use ordinary ground objectives;
- may cross shallow water, rough terrain and selected low obstacles.

Examples:

- Hover Scout;
- Razor Skimmer;
- Double Hover;
- Jet Scooter.

This distinction prevents every visually floating machine from bypassing map topology.

---

## 3. WALKERS

Walkers are ground units.

They may handle:

- rubble;
- uneven terrain;
- selected rough zones;

better than wheels, but do not ignore cliffs or walls.

---

## 4. TRANSPORT CAPACITY

Transport uses broad weight classes.

### Light

Workers, scouts, scooters and similarly small assets.

### Medium

Most normal vehicles.

### Heavy

Large industrial/heavy machines.

Only dedicated transports explicitly designed for heavy cargo can carry Heavy units.

### Faction transport identity

**Rock Raiders:** Tunnel Transport moves very heavy machinery but is expensive and conspicuous.

**Astronauts:** Recon Dropship offers the most practical conventional transport; Solar Explorer handles ground logistics.

**Aliens:** Mothership carries selected small craft; mobility normally comes from the units themselves.

**Martians:** Aero Tubes transfer only eligible small assets; Aero Skiff carries a tiny airborne payload.

---

## 5. AIR COUNTERPLAY

Every faction has reliable anti-air.

Air cannot invalidate terrain because:

- aircraft cannot mine or construct normal ground bases;
- aircraft cannot hold most territorial objectives by themselves;
- dedicated anti-air is available before advanced air dominates;
- major air units require Energy and production infrastructure;
- true-air structure-assault capability is deliberately limited;
- most heavy combat power remains on the ground;
- transport unloading requires valid ground locations;
- important static defenses punish unsupported air.

Air therefore changes approach geometry without making terrain irrelevant.

# PART X — TRANSFORMATION & MODULARITY RULES

A consistent game-wide rule now applies to physical LEGO transformation.

## 1. TACTICAL MODE SWITCH

Used only when two source configurations create materially different battlefield roles that require moment-to-moment choice.

Canonical examples:

- MX-41 Switch Fighter;
- ETX Alien Infiltrator;
- Red Planet Protector.

Each receives at most one primary transformation command.

---

## 2. DEPLOYMENT

Used where a machine changes from mobile to stationary operational behavior.

Examples:

- MT-201 Ultra-Drill Walker drill deployment;
- Solar Explorer service deployment;
- Martian positional apparatus.

Deployment is:

- visually obvious;
- temporarily vulnerable;
- strategically significant.

---

## 3. PRODUCTION / LOADOUT CHOICE

Used when modular physical components represent strategic preparation rather than combat micro.

Examples:

- T3-Trike Survey/Escort configuration;
- Mobile Mining Platform Ore Drill/Crystal Reaper configuration;
- Astronaut Sentinel modules;
- Alien Defense Node configuration.

Astronaut Mission Refit remains the major faction expression of this category.

---

## 4. AUTOMATIC PHYSICAL STATE

Used for source-faithful animation that does not justify a player-facing decision.

Examples:

- harvesting arms unfolding;
- cargo doors opening;
- drilling machinery lowering;
- Aero Tube loading;
- Mothership docking operations.

---

## 5. VISUAL-ONLY RECONFIGURATION

A source model may contain detachable or foldable details that are represented entirely through:

- idle animation;
- production animation;
- damage states;
- transport operation.

Physical LEGO transformability does not automatically generate an ability.

## Canonical implementation

**Rock Monsters are neutral hostile environmental creatures and campaign enemies.**

- a fifth playable faction;
- recruitable Raider units;
- a Rock Raider summon system.

- neutral resource guardians;
- cave hazards;
- optional high-value area defenders.

### Campaign use

Campaign scenarios can expand them into:

- cave threats;
- rescue complications;
- resource guardians;
- ecological story elements.

The creatures appearing in 4950 and 4990 therefore receive meaningful source coverage without distorting faction balance.

# PART XVI — UNIT OVERLAP AUDIT

## ROCK RAIDERS INTERNAL

### Hover Scout vs Rapid Rider

Hover Scout = information.

Rapid Rider = transport/logistics.

They are not interchangeable.

### Drill Craft vs Granite Grinder

Drill Craft = authored terrain utility.

Granite Grinder = anti-heavy combat.

The Granite Grinder can damage rock/structures but does not replace the Drill Craft's efficient route-opening role.

### Granite Grinder vs Chrome Crusher

Granite Grinder is a mid-game mobile anti-heavy walker.

Chrome Crusher is an advanced high-investment structure breaker.

Both remain because their timing, mobility and strategic commitment are fundamentally different.

---

# ASTRONAUT INTERNAL

### Rover vs T3-Trike

Rover = dedicated scout.

T3-Trike = early escort/frontline with configuration utility.

### Mono Jet vs Mission Fighter

Mono Jet = early information/harassment.

Mission Fighter = dedicated air superiority.

### Mission Fighter vs Switch Fighter

Mission Fighter wins conventional air fights.

Switch Fighter changes terrain access and target type but is less efficient in specialist combat.

### Mobile Mining Platform vs Armored Drilling Unit

Mining Platform = economy.

Armored Drilling Unit = combat.

This distinction is critical to prevent every Mars Mission drill becoming the same unit.

### Armored Drilling Unit vs Ultra-Drill Walker

Armored Drill = mobile frontline anti-heavy.

Ultra-Drill = deployed siege.

### Solar Explorer vs Operations Aircraft

Solar Explorer = forward ground service, Field Systems.

Operations Aircraft = advanced airborne information/support, Mission Systems.

Both lineages therefore remain relevant late.

---

# ALIEN INTERNAL

### Alien Jet vs Razor Skimmer

Alien Jet = true-air scout/interceptor.

Razor = ground-hover anti-light pressure.

### Alien Strike vs Infiltrator

Alien Strike = exclusively airborne structure assault.

Infiltrator = anti-heavy/detection frontline.

### Mothership vs normal combat craft

Mothership = strategic carrier/support.

It is deliberately inefficient as a replacement for dedicated attack craft.

---

# MARTIAN INTERNAL

### Double Hover vs Jet Scooter

Double Hover = scout.

Jet Scooter = combat harassment.

### Recon-Mech vs Protector

Recon-Mech = detection/AA.

Protector = anti-heavy positional anchor.

### Protector vs Excavation Searcher

Protector holds positions.

Searcher dismantles positions and manipulates units.

### Hover units vs Aero Skiff

Double Hover and Scooter remain ground-layer hover.

Aero Skiff is the faction's limited true-air asset.
